01 / Research & background

From theory
to computation.

My work sits at the intersection of numerical mathematics and high-performance computing.

I am a visiting researcher at the NATO Centre for Maritime Research and Experimentation (CMRE).

I focus on numerical and computational linear algebra: efficient algorithms, parallel solvers for differential equations, and methods for analyzing large matrices and datasets. I care about scientific software that is scalable, reusable, and maintainable.

I received my PhD in Computational Mathematics from the University of Padova, where I also held a research fellowship at the Department of Information Engineering. My experience includes the ROOT team at CERN, working on performance portability with SYCL, and the HPC/Cloud group at Leonardo Labs, developing cloud stacks for Big Data analytics.
